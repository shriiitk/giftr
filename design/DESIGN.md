## Brand & Style

This design system pairs the tactile precision of a physical catalog with modern, thoughtful utility. Built around the emotional landscape of gift-giving—curiosity, thoughtfulness, delight, and personal connection—it avoids cold algorithmic minimalism in favor of grounded, human-centered warmth. 

The aesthetic is **Warm Editorial Utility**: a hybrid of mid-century editorial layout disciplines and soft tactile elements. Clean grid structures, generous white space, and quiet structural lines create an organized framework, while organic earth tones and subtle micro-textures evoke high-grade paper stock, letterpress tags, and bespoke stationery. Interactions should feel deliberate, confident, and physical without veering into pastiche skeuomorphism.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an organic, tactile foundation that prioritizes optical warmth and legibility:

- **Foundation (`#FAF7F2`):** A soft, unbleached stone/cream paper-tone baseline that prevents screen glare and sets a quiet, welcoming stage.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure warm white utilized exclusively for cards, sheets, and interactive containers to lift content off the canvas.
- **Primary Accent (`#D9532F`):** Terracotta / burnt orange. Used for primary conversion points, active exploration filters, and core calls to action.
- **Secondary Accent (`#1B4D3E`):** Deep heritage forest green. Used for grounding accents, secondary highlights, curated recommendations, and trust markers.
- **Structural Borders (`#E8E2D9`):** Low-contrast warm gray-stone dividers and outlines mimicking folded paper creases and bookbinding margins.
- **Typography & Neutrals:**
  - Primary Content: `#1E293B` (deep slate) ensuring crisp, high-contrast readability without the harshness of pure black.
  - Secondary Content: `#64748B` (muted slate) for auxiliary descriptions, metadata, and placeholder states.
- **Decision Status System:**
  - Accepted / Saved: `#059669` (tactile emerald badge on light sage wash `#ECFDF5`).
  - Rejected / Dismissed: `#E11D48` (warm rose/coral badge on soft blush wash `#FFF1F2`).
  - Neutral / Under Review: `#475569` (slate token on neutral warm stone wash `#F1ECE4`).

## Typography

Typography balances architectural discipline with approachable, tactile warmth:

- **Headlines (`Epilogue`):** A modern, geometric sans-serif with subtle editorial idiosyncrasies. Its proportions recall high-end print design while remaining ultra-legible at punchy scales. Tighter letter spacing on display and large headlines evokes editorial titles.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, open, and friendly with humanist touches that maintain legibility even under high information density (pricing, specs, recipient traits).
- **Scale Rules:** Headings step down smoothly on mobile screens to preserve breathing room and prevent hyphenation or awkward line breaks. Form labels, captions, and micro-badges use medium-to-bold weights with slight tracking expansion to heighten crispness against warm surface backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adopts a fluid, responsive grid with a mobile-first bias that scales seamlessly up to compact desktop and laptop displays (max-width `1200px`):

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas margin. Filter pills horizontally scroll or wrap cleanly. Cards stack vertically with uniform `1rem` gaps.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 6-column fluid grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Gift idea cards reflow into 2-column modules; questionnaire forms adopt side-by-side input groupings.
- **Laptop (1024px – 1280px):** 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Accommodates an anchored 4-column filter/criteria drawer alongside an 8-column recommendation feed (or a 3-column gift discovery grid).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Component padding follows an 8pt base grid rhythm (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`). Whitespace is generous, allowing curated gift items to feel like distinguished catalog entries rather than dense search-engine results.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on physical materiality: crisp paper layers, subtle debossed contours, and gentle ambient light rather than artificial, fuzzy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Soft stone foundation (`#FAF7F2`), unshadowed, flat, absorbent.
- **Level 1 (Card & Container Surface):** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border (`#E8E2D9`) and an ultra-diffused, warm ambient resting shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Floating Cards / Dropdowns):** Subtle upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by an enhanced tactile shadow: `0 8px 16px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Overlays):** Deep ambient sheet shadow (`0 20px 25px -5px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`) seated over an editorial backdrop tint (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).
- **Inlaid / Debossed Elements (Form inputs & search fields):** Inset subtle boundary (`border: 1px solid #E8E2D9`) paired with a faint interior sink shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`) to emulate a soft press on heavy paper stock.

## Shapes

The shape system employs an organic, tailored roundness level (`2`) that feels friendly without losing structural rigor:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, utility panels, small cards, tooltips, and checkboxes.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Primary discovery cards, modal sheets, filter sections, and hero banners.
- **Accent Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for nested interactive trays and mobile bottom sheets.
- **Pill Form (Full Radius):** Dedicated to interactive filter pills, status badges, action tags, and floating primary conversion buttons. This contrast between structured rectilinear cards and organic pill controls reinforces tactile discoverability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-contrast terracotta fill (`#D9532F`), text `#FFFFFF`, fully rounded pill shape. Active state compresses gently (`scale(0.98)`). Focus ring: 2px offset with 2px solid `#D9532F`.
- **Secondary:** Deep forest green outline or subtle tint (`#1B4D3E` text, border 1.5px solid `#1B4D3E`, background transparent or `#FAF7F2` on hover).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Slate text (`#1E293B`), no border, hover background `#E8E2D9` at 40% opacity.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Fully rounded pills with a 1px border (`#E8E2D9`) and white background (`#FFFFFF`).
- Unselected state uses `#64748B` text.
- Selected state shifts to `#1B4D3E` background with `#FAF7F2` text, or terracotta accent depending on filter tier, instantly conveying active state with tactile clarity.

### Cards (Gift Discovery)
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid `#E8E2D9` border, `rounded-lg` corner radii.
- Media container features a soft neutral inset frame with subtle image zoom on hover.
- Header holds gift title, price, and matching confidence score. Footer incorporates quick-action icon buttons (heart, bookmark, dismiss) using high-contrast slate outlines.

### Decision Status Badges
- Compact pill geometries with small, bold text (`label-sm`).
- **Accepted:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, with a miniature solid emerald dot indicator.
- **Rejected:** Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#E11D48`, with a miniature warm coral cross indicator.
- **Neutral / Draft:** Background `#F1ECE4`, text `#475569`, providing unobtrusive status context.

### Form Inputs & Privacy Focus
- Debossed card inputs with `#FFFFFF` background, `#E8E2D9` border, and `#1E293B` text.
- Focused state features a crisp `#D9532F` perimeter ring with 0 0 0 3px `#D9532F1A`.
- **Privacy Indicator:** Includes a dedicated lock/shield badge (`#1B4D3E` accent with `#E8E2D9` fill) adjacent to sensitive queries (recipient budget, relationship dynamics, personal quirks) accompanied by micro-copy confirming private local evaluation.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px` with 4px border-radius (checkbox) or circle (radio).
- Inactive: `#E8E2D9` border with `#FFFFFF` fill.
- Active: `#D9532F` fill with crisp white internal check or pip, offering immediate, unambiguous confirmation.
